# Stage 5: Behavioral Change — Player Objective

## What the player is trying to achieve

Execute the surrender. Move Bato from commitment to physical follow-through, with the deal holding and his psychological stability maintained through the transition.

## What needs to happen

Bato has made his choice. Now he needs reassurance that the deal holds, that the process is what was agreed, and that he has a continuity point — someone reachable who can answer questions as he goes through the surrender.

## Key player moves

- **Confirm all deal terms before execution** — all protections re-stated, logistics clear.
- **Provide step-by-step logistics** — what happens, when, where, who is there, what he needs to bring.
- **Acknowledge the difficulty** — "You're not walking into a celebration. You're walking into something hard. But the process is what we agreed."
- **Maintain continuity of contact** — remain reachable during the final hours. Don't disappear on him.
- **Confirm ally protection and family access** — these are the things he needs to hear re-stated as final reassurance.
- **Let him frame the decision as principled** — support his narrative that this was a choice, not capitulation.

## What to avoid

- Breaking deal terms before or during surrender — different conditions than agreed will cause him to run
- Disappearing or being unreachable during final hours
- Dismissing his fear — "You'll be fine, don't worry" without acknowledgment
- Pushing immediate physical surrender before the legal framework is established
- Exposing the deal publicly before it's finalized — media reports "Bato negotiating surrender" before it's done

## Outcome signals

- **Complete:** Bato executes surrender, deal holds, process begins
- **Partial:** Bato commits but with significant doubt or last-minute renegotiation
- **Failed:** Bato runs, collapses, or deal collapses
