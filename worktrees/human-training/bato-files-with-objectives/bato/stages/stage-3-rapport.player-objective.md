# Stage 3: Rapport — Player Objective

## What the player is trying to achieve

Move Bato from trust-based engagement to active evaluation of options. He must feel that he has genuine agency — that you are exploring possibilities with him, not steering him toward a predetermined outcome.

## What needs to happen

Bato must see you as a collaborative problem-solver, not a person with an agenda. He is now invested enough to think creatively about options, but he is watching for signs that his input won't actually shape the outcome.

## Key player moves

- **Present multiple realistic options** — not just "surrender" but alternatives with honest tradeoffs.
- **Acknowledge what he would have to give up** — without minimizing it. "You'd have to accept that you can't go back to the life you had before. That's real. That's a cost."
- **Address ally protection as a legitimate concern** — "The people who helped you — their exposure would be a factor in any agreement." This is often the final unlock.
- **Speak honestly about costs and benefits** of each option — "If you stay hidden, here's what's likely to happen. If you surrender, here's what that looks like."
- **Give concrete, specific proposals** — processes, timelines, legal mechanisms — not vague reassurance.
- **Let him shape the conversation** — he proposes conditions, asks for clarifications, pushes back.

## What to avoid

- Pushing a single preferred outcome without alternatives
- Making him feel managed — as if you've already decided what he should do
- Vague reassurances — "It'll be okay," "Don't worry about that"
- Making commitments on behalf of other parties that cannot be verified
- Applying new arrest pressure while he's exploring options
- Breaking confidentiality before a deal is finalized

## Stage transition signal

Bato has moved to Stage 4 when he begins to ask "what if" questions and explore scenarios actively. He says things like "If I surrendered, would I be able to..." or "What would happen if we did X instead of Y?" He is moving from "I can't" to "what if."
