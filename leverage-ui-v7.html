How Leverage Works
Five-stage FBI de-escalation framework. Build trust to unlock leverage opportunities. Don't let pressure hit 6 stars.
The 5 Stages
1️⃣Active Listening — Mirror their words, don't talk over them
2️⃣Empathy — Name the emotion: "You feel cornered"
3️⃣Rapport — Use their name, find common ground
4️⃣Influence — Open questions: "What would it look like if this ended differently?"
5️⃣Behavioral Change — Get "that's right" not "you're right"
